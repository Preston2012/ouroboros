# Supporting Documents

Material that sits alongside the architecture rather than inside it.

**[SIX_REPAIRS.md](./SIX_REPAIRS.md)** is a one-page near-term program: six changes to American self-government drawn from the architecture, each of which stands on its own without requiring agreement to anything else. It is written to be read cold, by someone who has never heard of this project, in about three minutes.

**[THE_CONVENTION_WE_NEVER_HELD.md](./THE_CONVENTION_WE_NEVER_HELD.md)** is the working brief behind those six. It carries the evidence for each one graded honestly, the legal reality of where each can and cannot be adopted, the relationship of this work to the existing Article V movement, and the author's conflict of interest.

**[SOURCES_AND_SUPPORT.md](./SOURCES_AND_SUPPORT.md)** is the sourcing appendix. Every claim of public support with its source, graded A through C, with advocacy-commissioned polling labeled as such and the two repairs that have no polling at all identified as having none.

**[kit/](./kit/)** is the usable material: model ordinance text for the three repairs a local body can lawfully adopt, a brief on each of the six, an objection-and-answer sheet, and a deck someone can present without the author in the room. Nothing in it requires permission or contact.

These documents are American-specific. The architecture in the parent directory is not. Nothing here is a campaign, an organization, or a request. It is material anyone can take from.
